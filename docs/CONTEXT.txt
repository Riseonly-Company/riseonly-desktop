The canon, adapted for this repo. The originals live in ../../riseonly-ios and
remain the source of truth for anything these do not cover.

  ARCHITECTURE_BOUNDARIES.md  the three rules CI enforces, and every deliberate
                              deviation from the iOS reference, with reasons
  DEPENDENCIES.md             every external crate, why it is here, what replaces
                              it if it dies
  STORE_ARCHITECTURE.md       the store triad in Rust ownership terms
  PERFORMANCE_GUIDE.md        the performance contract, restated for desktop
  ENGINE.md                   the RiseEngine contract

Read the reference documents in full before changing store or engine code. These
adaptations tell you what is different here; they do not repeat what is the same.
