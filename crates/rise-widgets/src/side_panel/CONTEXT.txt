================================================================================
rise-widgets/side_panel — the extra column
================================================================================

  panel_gestures.rs  resize, dismiss and swipe-back. Pure and tested.
  side_panel_ui.rs   the entity, the grip and the frame.

WHAT IT IS A PORT OF

  The reference shows comments in a `BottomSheetUi` with snap fractions of 0.56
  and 0.92 and a drag-down dismissal. A desktop window has no bottom to drag
  towards, so the same three affordances are ROTATED onto the horizontal axis:
  the snap range becomes a width range, the drag handle becomes the panel's inner
  edge, and the dismiss threshold becomes a width below which letting go closes
  it. The numbers are ours; the RELATIONSHIP is the reference's.

THE TWO DETAILS THAT ARE EASY TO GET WRONG

  A drag may go BELOW the floor. Clamping during the gesture would make a
  dismissal happen with no warning; the floor is applied on RELEASE.

  The width is restored BEFORE the dismissal is announced, so a panel reopened
  later opens at a usable width rather than at the sliver it was dragged to.

WHY THE RELEASE IS CAUGHT TWICE

  `on_mouse_up` fires when the pointer is over the panel and `on_mouse_up_out`
  when it is not — and the common case, dragging the edge left until the panel
  closes, ends with the pointer outside it. Only one of the two is not enough.

  Both are guarded on there being a drag in progress. Without that guard every
  ordinary click inside the panel would settle its width, and some would close it.

THE PAGE STACK IS THE PANEL'S, NOT ITS CONTENT'S

  `PanelPages` is generic and the root can never be popped. Anything the panel
  hosts gets comments-and-their-replies for free, and Esc has a defined meaning
  at every depth: pop a page if there is one, close the panel if there is not.
