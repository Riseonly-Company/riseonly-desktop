WHAT THIS CRATE OWNS
  RequestId and its allocator. The id packs (logical_ms << 20 | sequence) and
  never regresses when the wall clock jumps backwards, so correlation stays
  unique across a system time change.

  Generation / GenerationCounter, and StaleGuard: the captured identity of the
  work that started an async operation. StaleGuard checks account, epoch, scope
  AND generation together, because checking the scope alone loses the ABA case
  (screen A closes, B opens, A opens again, and a stale result from the first A
  looks valid).

  AccountId, ScopeId, AccountEpoch.

WHAT IT MUST NEVER DO
  Depend on gpui, on any OS crate, on rise-engine, or on anything desktop.
  This crate must stay compilable for a future FFI or wasm consumer. CI enforces
  it (scripts/check-boundaries.py).
