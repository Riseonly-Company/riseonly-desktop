WHAT THIS IS
  A binary, not a library. It owns the shell and every feature module. Anything
  reusable and layered lives in ../crates.

LAYOUT
  src/main.rs      installs the theme, opens the window, hands over
  src/app/         root view, router, tab chrome
  src/app/router/  RootRoute -> screen. The match is exhaustive on purpose:
                   adding a route fails the build until someone renders it.
  src/core/        account scope, session activity, active screens, engine
                   bridge, shared utilities. Plumbing for THIS application —
                   anything another product could reuse belongs in ../crates.
  src/modules/     22 feature modules

MODULES ARE ORGANISATION, NOT ISOLATION
  A module groups the pages, components and stores of one product area. Modules
  may reference each other directly — they are not independent products, and
  putting traits between them to pretend otherwise buys nothing.

  What they may NOT do is reach past their layer: a view calls only its
  InteractionsStore, stores never touch rise_engine directly, and engine code
  never touches gpui. CI checks all three.

  There is no sai and no compatibility layer. A store issues an intent, the
  module's engine/ owns the data and speaks to rise-engine. That is the only
  path to the backend.

WHAT DOES NOT BELONG HERE
  Anything another application could reuse. Design tokens, primitives, widgets,
  the data engine, OS seams and navigation mechanics are crates.

  #[cfg(target_os)] — never. Platform differences go through rise-platform.
