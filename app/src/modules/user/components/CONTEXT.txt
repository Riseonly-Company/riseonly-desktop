================================================================================
modules/user/components — the header and the tabs
================================================================================

  profile_top/      ProfileTop, ported from ProfileTop.swift.
  profile_content/  ProfileContentTab and the goal and plan rows.

ONE HANDLER, NOT THREE

  Same rule as the post card: one `ProfileTopAction` enum and one closure, so a
  second call site does not re-declare a list of callbacks.

WHAT THE HEADER DRAWS AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

  Banner, avatar, name with its badges, tag, bio, the four-segment stats bar,
  the Rise pills on your own profile, and the follow button on someone else's.

  The reference also puts a message button, a share button, an edit pencil, a
  story ring with its composer, highlights, and a featured track up here. None of
  their destinations exist yet, and a button that does nothing is worse than an
  absent one — the stats segments are drawn as text rather than buttons for the
  same reason.

THE STATIC GLASS IS DELIBERATE, IN BOTH PROJECTS

  ProfileTop.swift replaced its live glass with painted fills and strokes,
  because the whole subtree moves while the header collapses and a material
  recomposites every frame. The port keeps that decision: these surfaces are
  `bg._200` with a border, never `GlassPanel`. The materials belong to chrome
  that stays still.

  Same reason the premium badge is drawn rather than animated (BadgeUi says so):
  gpui has no damage regions, so anything looping repaints the window.

THE AVATAR HANGS, IT DOES NOT OVERLAP

  A negative top margin on the identity row pulls it up over the banner by
  `avatar_hang`. The reference offsets the row inside a ZStack and pads the
  container by the same amount; the effect is identical and the layout has one
  pass instead of two.
