THE REPOSITORY IS A TASK ON A CHANNEL, NOT A MUTEX

  PHASES.txt Phase 2 records why, and it applies here unchanged: the canon's
  guarantees are ORDERING guarantees. A mutex gives exclusion without ordering,
  so two toggles on one post could interleave their optimistic write and their
  reconciliation and settle on the wrong answer.

GENERATIONS

  Bumped per scope on every first load and on every reset. A response carrying an
  old generation is DROPPED rather than installed: the account may have changed
  while the request was out, and installing that page would show one account's
  feed under another's session. `SetIdentity` resets whenever the user id moves,
  which is also what stops a mutation being stamped with the previous user.

WHAT IS NOT HERE YET

  No RiseStore persistence. The feed is memory-only, so a relaunch refetches.
  That is a real gap against section 12 of the store canon — cache-first is
  implemented across the ACTIVATE path (an already-loaded feed is not re-fetched)
  but not across a cold start. The entity/view-window plumbing to fix it exists
  in rise-engine; wiring it is its own change, not a line in this one.

  No realtime. post_liked / post_favorited / post_created events are published by
  the gateway and nothing here subscribes, so a like from another device shows up
  on the next refresh rather than immediately.
